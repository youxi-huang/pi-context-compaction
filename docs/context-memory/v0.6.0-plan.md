# v0.6.0 footprint plan

Status: proposed. The decisions M1 to M8 below wait for the maintainer; nothing
in this plan authorizes a provider run, a release, or a change to the resident
fingerprint or evaluation pin. Each of those waits for the maintainer, as in the
[v0.5.0 plan](v0.5.0-plan.md). The plan covers the 0.6.0 milestone in the
[roadmap](roadmap.md#planned-minor-releases): the evidence-based refinement of
the v0.2.3 size tiers and of writer overhead, reversible deterministic context
reduction, rendered-note and retrieval candidates, and the acceptance bench that
0.5.3 bequeathed. It also carries one obligation to a later release: 0.8.0's
opt-in reduction fallback after a failed compaction is taken only once 0.6.0 has
shown its reductions safe, so the reduction contracts here are written to be
judged, not merely shipped.

## What the evidence says

The cost that remains is tokens, not the extension's time. A request carries
about 1 ms of preview processing at 40 checkpoints, and the
[cost plan](cost-plan.md) already removed the `context_note` declaration from
B-off sessions. What Z1 measured and the benches confirm:

- A checkpoint's rendered summary grows with its lineage, not its facts: at 1,
  10 and 40 checkpoints the summary was 436, 1,234 and 2,220 bytes while the
  fact text stayed at 202
  ([Z1](validation.md#z1-preview-request-and-checkpoint-cost-official-pi-130-2026-10-18-pdt)).
  The fixed text — headings, guidance, lineage with its repeated state lines —
  is what accumulates.
- A committed note condenses well: median 4,236 to 4,536 bytes, 5 to 6% of the
  bytes it replaces, across the 0.5.3 and sources-repair benches. What no report
  yet separates is the writer's own spend per committed byte, the usage of
  failed, cancelled and discarded calls, calls whose usage report is missing,
  and the provider's cache writes. The bench records usage per call and
  `sumMemoryUsage` already carries `cacheWrite` and `cacheWrite1h`; the columns
  exist in the data but not in a required report.
- The prompt cache dominates writer cost. On DeepSeek with `--cache warm` the
  writer reads 96% of its input from cache; the 0.5.0 cache fix cut writer cost
  by about 70%. A reduction that rewrites an early entry invalidates every
  cached token after it, so a smaller context can cost more than it saves — the
  roadmap makes this a condition, and this plan makes it a report column and an
  eligibility rule, not a remark.
- Only direct Anthropic catalog models declare a provider cache lifetime on the
  supported hosts; DeepSeek and the OpenAI models declare none (the Z2
  investigation in the cost plan). An idle-based reduction trigger cannot read a
  lifetime it is not given.
- The 0.5.3 Luna criterion is unjudged, not failed. Through the relay the
  maintainer chose, Luna committed 47 of 64 with 8 relay transport failures,
  and the relay scales its usage counts, so the cache-share criterion could not
  be read; the sources-repair run beside v0.5.3 answered too few Luna attempts
  (12 and 10 across its two rounds, both builds together) to judge anything
  ([acceptance benches](validation.md#053-acceptance-benches-on-deepseek-v41-flash-and-gpt-6-luna-through-a-relay-official-pi-130-2026-10-20-to-21-utc);
  [sources-repair candidate](validation.md#sources-repair-candidate-beside-v053-through-a-relay-deepseek-v41-flash-and-gpt-6-luna-official-pi-130-2026-10-21-utc)).
  The writer instruction that names every fact section and the bounded sources
  repair are already in the preview source with synthetic regressions
  (`preview/sources-repair.ts`, tested in
  `test/context-memory-preview-quote.test.ts` and the `sources-repair-refused`
  and `sources-repair-commits` scenarios). What remains is a bench on a channel
  that can report true usage and deliver enough answers.
- The v0.2.3 tiers are untouched intuition: note budgets step at 80,000,
  200,000 and 400,000 source tokens (`selectNoteBudget` in the shared
  `config.ts`). No measurement has yet said whether those boundaries or
  allowances fit the notes real benches produce. `config.ts` is fingerprinted,
  so a refinement cannot land there without the first deliberate gate; the
  preview carries its own copy where measurement supports a change, as N1 had
  it carry `searchTerms`.
- The transformation points exist. The preview already handles Pi's `context`
  and `context_with_system` events and returns the messages unchanged
  (`controller.context`), and the session writer builds its prefix from the
  same session projection (`buildSessionProjection` plus `convertToLlm` in
  `controller.compact`). A view-level reduction therefore has exactly two call
  sites, and one pure function can serve both, so the main model and the writer
  share one cached prefix.
- The guards that must keep holding are explicit: `controller.context` requires
  the committed checkpoint's summary in context and complete tool pairing
  (`assertToolPairs`), and `beforeRequest` requires the summary in the provider
  payload. Any reduction or re-rendering must keep those checks true against a
  deterministically derived expectation, never weaken them.

## Decisions

Proposed to the maintainer. On 2026-10-22 the maintainer confirmed the scope
and procedure rows — M1, M3 and M4 — and that every remaining work package may
proceed except the provider benches themselves, which wait until the
maintainer schedules them. Until those benches run, the measurement-dependent
rows stay at their conservative defaults: M2's switches stay off, M5's
proposed bounds stand as named constants, M6 waits for tier evidence, M7's
shipping call waits for its measured cost, and M8 keeps `full` as the
default.

| ID | Proposal | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M1 | 0.6.0 changes only the independent preview | New code lives under `preview/`, plus the bench, scenarios and `eval/preview/`. The shared `notes.ts`, `history.ts`, `events.ts`, `config.ts` and `identity.ts` stay unchanged, so the resident `build.ts` fingerprint and the `eval/runtime.ts` pin do not move |
| M2 | Reductions and excerpts ship behind their own switches, off by default during measurement | `previewReduction` and `previewExcerpts` in `pi-context-memory.json`, following the `previewPreparation` pattern: an invalid value disables the feature without touching requests or the failure contract. Whether either defaults on at release is decided from the WP1 report, by the maintainer |
| M3 | A provider bench qualifies only on a channel that reports the provider's own usage — input, output and cache reads unscaled, cache writes where the provider reports them — and passes the session writer's cache key through | As measured, the Codex subscription qualifies and the 0.5.3 relay does not (scaled counts). A run in which transport failures exceed one attempt in ten, or that answers fewer than three quarters of its writer attempts per build, is recorded but decides no criterion |
| M4 | The channel and budget for each provider bench | Each run's request, time and spending envelope is reported to the maintainer and approved before the first request, as N3 had it. No provider call is part of executing this plan |
| M5 | The reduction timing bounds | Proposed openings: an entry first reduces when the estimated tokens that follow it on the branch are no more than the tokens the reduction removes, so one reduction never writes more cache than it saves on its own request; or when the gap between consecutive records exceeds the model's declared cache lifetime, with one hour assumed where none is declared. Final values are set from the WP1 report |
| M6 | Tier refinement is preview-local | If WP1's per-tier breakdown supports changing a boundary or allowance, the preview carries its own budget table; `config.ts` and the resident behavior stay. No change without the measurement |
| M7 | The instruction-authority first step rides WP3 | The rendered note showing each fact's cited roles, computed by the host, is a WP3 candidate measured with the others, as the roadmap assigns; whether it ships in 0.6.0 or waits for the authority release is the maintainer's call once its token cost is known |
| M8 | WP4's low-detail default | Whether `context_history`'s read returns low detail first by default, or only on request, is decided from the probe measurements; a default that raises calls per question without raising accuracy is rejected by the retrieval gate |

Design rules this plan adopts:

| ID | Rule | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G1 | A reduction is a pure, deterministic function of the records | Recomputed on every call, never stored; the same records always produce the same bytes, across requests, reopens and process restarts. This shape is required so 0.7.0 can lift the reduction into the host-neutral core unchanged — it may depend on records, never on host state, wall-clock time or anything outside them |
| G2 | Eligibility is monotone in the records | Appending records never un-reduces an entry, so the cached prefix is stable between requests. The idle trigger reads the gap between consecutive record timestamps, not the clock, so it stays a function of the records: the first entry appended after a long gap proves the gap to every later call |
| G3 | Nothing in the session file is rewritten | Originals stay on disk untouched, unlike hosts that shrink stored entries in place; `context_history` reads a reduced entry back in full; deleting the reduction code restores the full view |
| G4 | One function, two call sites | The same reduction is applied to the `context` projection and to the writer's session prefix, so the writer continues the session's cache and the note condenses a smaller input |
| G5 | Reduction never separates a tool call from its result | The call stays verbatim; the result's text is replaced by the one line. `assertToolPairs`, the checkpoint-presence checks and the payload guard hold on the reduced view, compared against expectations derived by the same pure functions |
| G6 | User messages, assistant text, checkpoint summaries and `context_note` records are never reduced | Both roadmap candidates are tool results. Quotable text and rulings stay verbatim; a reduced entry remains citable by ID but its full text is only in the records, where validation reads it |
| G7 | Stored formats stay as they are | No new checkpoint, note or marker field; the stored summary keeps its existing rendering byte for byte. v0.4.x and v0.5.x checkpoints reopen unchanged, and a v0.6.0 checkpoint opens under v0.5.3 |
| G8 | Measure before mechanism | The WP1 report exists and a baseline is recorded before any WP2 to WP4 candidate is built; every candidate runs through the same report |
| G9 | Paging counts against the saving | Tokens spent reading reduced or compacted content back in, excerpts included, are retrieval usage, reported separately and subtracted from any claimed reduction |
| G10 | No rendered text changes with the time of rendering | Nothing in a projection may depend on when it is rendered, only on the records it is rendered from |

## Work packages

### WP0 Close the inherited 0.5.3 acceptance

The code is merged: the writer instruction names every fact section, next steps
included, and the compaction's one repair may add sources to at most five facts
when missing sources are the candidate's only fault against the schema — add
only, withdraw nothing, every commit check runs again
(`preview/sources-repair.ts`, `SOURCES_REPAIR_MAX_FACTS`,
`sourcesRepairEligible`, `applySourcesRepair`; regressions in
`test/context-memory-preview-quote.test.ts` and the stock-Pi scenarios). The
roadmap's acceptance criterion applies to them unchanged: in writer benches on
the same day, Luna at `max` commits at least nine notes in ten and reads most
of its writer input from cache, DeepSeek commits as often as the v0.5.3 release
build, and probes on the committed notes show no loss of recovery.

What this plan adds is the measurement-channel contract, because two relay
rounds produced runs that could not be judged:

- The channel reports the provider's own usage: input, output and cache reads
  unscaled, and cache writes where the provider reports them. The Codex
  subscription reports its own counts; the 0.5.3 relay marked its counts as
  scaled and reported a nine-token request as thousands.
- The channel passes the session writer's `prompt_cache_key` through, so the
  cache-share criterion measures the preview, not the transport.
- The run answers enough to decide: transport failures above one attempt in
  ten, or fewer than three quarters of a build's writer attempts answered,
  record the run as not deciding the criterion (M3). The 0.5.3 relay failed 8
  of 64, and in the follow-up each build's runs had only 3 to 9 of 21 to 24
  attempts answered; neither could separate one refusal in six from one in
  sixty.
- Both builds run side by side, started together with the same bench settings,
  at least two runs each, as the runbook's evidence rule requires.

The bench itself is a separate step: the maintainer names the channel and
approves the request, time and cost limits before the first request (M4).
Nothing in WP0 executes a provider call as part of this plan. If the criterion
is met, the result closes the 0.5.3 follow-up inside 0.6.0; if it is not met on
a qualifying channel, that is a finding about the writer, recorded with
denominators, and the maintainer decides what follows.

Completion: the channel contract recorded here; one qualifying paired bench and
probe run recorded in `validation.md` as provider evidence with denominators,
judged against the unchanged criterion.

### WP1 Measurement before mechanism

The token-overhead report every candidate must run through before any candidate
is built. It extends the bench (`scripts/context-memory-preview-bench.mjs`) and
its `summary.json` rather than adding a new tool, and every column is computed
offline from the loopback provider's deterministic usage, so the report's
arithmetic is a zero-budget regression. Required columns, per run and per
fixture cut:

- **Writer tokens and note size against the records they replace.** The bench
  already reports note bytes against condensed bytes; add the writer's total
  input, cached input, output and reasoning tokens per committed checkpoint,
  and the same figures per note byte, broken down by the v0.2.3 source-size
  tier the cut falls in, so M6 has its evidence.
- **Retrieval usage, separately.** `context_history` calls, their tokens and
  the tokens of what they returned, by operation, never pooled with writer
  usage (G9).
- **Known usage from failed, cancelled and discarded work.** Refused attempts'
  writer calls, declined tool-call continuations, repairs that did not commit,
  and discarded or failed preparations, read from the call journal and the
  per-attempt rows; a crashed or timed-out unit's recorded usage stays in this
  column rather than vanishing.
- **Calls with missing usage reports, identified.** A count (`usageMissing`)
  and the phases they occurred in, so a channel that swallows usage is visible
  in the report instead of deflating the totals — the 0.5.3 relay made this a
  requirement, not a nicety.
- **Provider-reported cache writes.** `cacheWrite` and `cacheWrite1h` as a
  required column beside cache reads, because a smaller context that
  invalidates the cache can cost more than it saves. Where a provider reports
  no cache writes, the column says so instead of reading zero. A candidate
  whose cache writes rise by more than its input falls has not reduced
  anything.
- **Page-back cost.** Tokens spent reading reduced content back in — the
  `context_history` reads that name a reduced entry, and WP4 excerpts — counted
  against the candidate's saving in one net column.

`/compaction-status` gains content-free counts for the new mechanisms (entries
reduced, estimated tokens removed, read-backs, excerpts appended and unused),
so a bench row and a user's session report the same facts.

Regressions, in `test/context-memory-preview-bench-report.test.ts` and the
existing bench loopback runs: each column computed from scripted usage; a call
without usage counted under `usageMissing`, not as zero; discarded preparation
usage retained; the net column charges page-backs; tier assignment at the
boundaries.

Completion: the report merged with offline regressions; the current preview
(the WP0 source, which changes no mechanism) run through it on the loopback
provider and recorded as the offline baseline shape. The provider baseline is
taken together with the first candidate bench under M4, not as a separate
spend.

### WP2 Deterministic reversible reductions

Two candidates, both from the roadmap and both without any model call,
following the deterministic tiers of Better Compact:

- **R-A, the superseded file read:** an earlier read of a file that was read
  again later on the branch — same tool, same target, a later result present.
  The latest read stays in full.
- **R-B, the consumed tool result:** a tool result from a completed turn, after
  which at least one later assistant turn finished.

Each eligible result is replaced, in the view only, by one line naming the
tool, its target, the first line of its result and the record ID, and saying
that `context_history` reads it back in full. The tool call of the pair stays
verbatim (G5); nothing in the session file is rewritten (G3).

Eligibility is a commit-grade contract, every clause a pure predicate over the
records:

1. **Active requests kept.** No entry inside an unfinished request or the
   current turn is touched (`inProgressRequests` defines the set).
2. **Pairing complete.** The entry is a tool result whose call is present; the
   reduction replaces the result's text and never removes or splits a pair.
3. **Sole evidence kept.** An entry that is the only source cited by any later
   committed checkpoint fact or saved work-note fact stays in full, as does any
   `isError` result and any entry outside G6's reducible set.
4. **Timing.** An entry first becomes reduced only when few tokens follow it on
   the branch — proposed: no more than the tokens the reduction removes — or
   when the records show an idle gap longer than the provider's cache lifetime
   between it and the next entry (M5, G2). Both tests read only the records, so
   the function stays pure; once reduced, always reduced.
5. **Determinism.** The reduction is recomputed on every call from the branch
   snapshot, never stored; the same records produce the same bytes, and the
   reduced prefix is byte-stable across requests, reopens and restarts.

The function lives in `preview/reduction.ts` and is applied in exactly two
places: `controller.context` (and `context_with_system`) for the main model,
and the prefix construction in `controller.compact` and the preparation path
for the writer (G4). The guards adjust without weakening: the token estimate in
`context` measures the reduced projection; the checkpoint-presence and
`beforeRequest` checks are unchanged, since summaries are never reduced; the
source snapshot, hashes and disk readback keep reading originals, so a
checkpoint commit is untouched by reduction. A reduction is not a compaction:
it writes no marker, has no failure state of its own, and a failed compaction
blocks exactly as before — the opt-in fallback that reuses these reductions is
0.8.0's decision, taken only after this package's gates have shown them safe.

The pure-function shape is required, stated here explicitly, so that 0.7.0 can
lift `reduction.ts` into the host-neutral core unchanged: it takes records and
a model's declared cache lifetime, returns the reduced view, and knows nothing
of Pi.

Regressions, in `test/context-memory-preview-reduction.test.ts`:

- determinism: same records, same bytes, across two calls, a reopen and a
  simulated restart;
- monotonicity: appending turns never un-reduces, and the reduced prefix of the
  longer branch extends that of the shorter byte for byte;
- each eligibility clause: active requests, pairing, sole evidence (checkpoint
  fact, work-note fact, `isError`), the G6 exclusions;
- both timing triggers, including a model with no declared lifetime and the
  idle gap read from record timestamps;
- the one-line form: tool, target, first result line, record ID;
- `context_history` returns the full original of a reduced entry;
- the writer prefix and the context projection reduce identically;
- a compaction over a reduced view commits, validates and reopens, and its
  checkpoint equals one written without reduction active;
- `previewReduction` off, invalid, and absent behave identically;
- scenario checks `reduction-stable-prefix` and `reduction-readback` in
  `scripts/context-memory-preview-scenarios.mjs`.

Measurement: `eval/preview` gains a fixture (P4) whose sessions carry repeated
file reads and long consumed tool results between checkpoints, with gold
answers that live inside reduced entries, under a new fixtures version with its
manifest; the frozen F-series is untouched. The WP1 report on P1 to P4 gives
the candidate's net saving including cache writes and page-backs, and the
probes give its recovery cost.

Completion: regressions and strict stock-Pi acceptance pass on Pi 1.3.0, 1.2.0
and 1.1.0; the offline WP1 report shows the reduced view smaller with page-back
charged; probe accuracy on the loopback runs is unchanged. Provider evidence
follows M4.

### WP3 Rendered-note candidates

The stored checkpoint keeps its existing rendering byte for byte (G7); these
candidates change only the projection the model sees, derived from the stored
note and lineage by a pure function, and the recovery-presence guards compare
against that same derivation, so the payload stays bound to the committed note.
A v0.5.x preview reopening the session renders the stored summary as today.

Candidates, measured one at a time with the WP1 report:

- **Older failed paths and reasons as one line each with their source IDs.**
  Current state, standing instructions and next steps render in full, as now;
  a failed path or reason whose sources precede the previous checkpoint renders
  as its one-line fact and IDs without quotation or guidance text, and the
  newest entries keep their full rendering. `context_history` and the typed
  list already read these back.
- **Lineage without repetition.** Z1 showed the fixed text — headings, guidance
  and lineage with repeated state lines — growing from 234 to 2,018 bytes over
  40 checkpoints while facts stayed constant; the candidate renders guidance
  once and folds repeated lineage state lines, with the lineage IDs kept.
- **Cited roles (M7).** Each fact shows the roles of the records it cites —
  user message, tool output or assistant text — computed by the host from the
  records, never claimed by the note. This candidate adds tokens; it is
  measured here because the roadmap assigns its measurement to 0.6.0's
  rendering candidates, and whether it ships is the maintainer's call. The
  broader instruction-authority protocol keeps its own release.

No rendered text may change with the time of rendering (G10), so the projection
carries no ages, no "now" and no relative times.

Regressions, in `test/context-memory-preview-render.test.ts`: the stored
summary unchanged byte for byte under every candidate; the projection derived
equal from a fresh render and a reopen; the guards accept the projected
rendering and still refuse a tampered one; one-line folding keeps source IDs;
a v0.4.x checkpoint renders; role tags match the records for each role.

Completion: regressions and acceptance pass; the WP1 report gives each
candidate's per-request saving at 1, 10 and 40 checkpoints offline; recovery
cost waits for the M4 probes.

### WP4 Retrieval candidates

The 0.5.0 typed view already lists by kind with superseded marks, deduplicates
across checkpoints and returns original excerpts; these candidates make its
cost visible and its paging lazier, in `preview/history-view.ts` and the
`context_history` tool in `preview/extension.ts`.

- **State the cost before the read.** Every search hit and list item carries
  `readTokens`, the preview's estimate of reading its entry in full, so the
  model can decide against a read it does not need.
- **Low detail first.** `read` gains a `detail` level: low returns a bounded
  excerpt with the full cost stated; `full` reads as today. Whether low becomes
  the default is M8, judged by the retrieval gate — fewer calls with lower
  accuracy is a failure, not a saving.
- **Mark results already in context.** A hit whose entry is in the active
  projection, unreduced, is marked `inContext`, so the model does not page in
  what it already holds. A reduced entry is not marked, since only its one line
  is in context.
- **The LCM-style excerpt.** When the user's prompt names an exact identifier
  that no longer appears in the projection — an identifier-shaped term, matched
  deterministically, present in compacted or reduced entries but absent from
  the current view — the extension appends a short original excerpt at the end
  of the context, after the user's message, so the cached prefix is untouched.
  The excerpt is labelled as historical evidence with its record ID and role,
  never as an instruction; it is bounded (proposed: at most two excerpts of at
  most 200 estimated tokens each per request), passed through a deterministic
  secret filter that withholds a matching excerpt entirely, naming only the
  record ID; it enters no session record, and its tokens count as retrieval
  (G9). Excerpts the model did not need — appended but cited by no answer —
  are a required report column.

Regressions, in `test/context-memory-preview-history-view.test.ts` and
`test/context-memory-preview-excerpt.test.ts`: `readTokens` against entry
bytes; low detail bounded with the cost stated; `inContext` marks, including
a reduced entry unmarked; identifier matching hits only exact terms absent from
the view; the excerpt's label, ID, role, bound and position; the secret filter
withholding on each planted pattern; nothing appended when the identifier is in
context or the prompt names none; excerpts absent from the session file;
`previewExcerpts` off by default. P4's probe set includes questions whose
answers name identifiers only reduced entries hold, so needed and unneeded
excerpts are both measured.

Completion: regressions and acceptance pass; the offline probe runs report
calls per question, `readTokens` honesty (stated cost within the page bound of
actual), and unneeded excerpts. Provider evidence follows M4.

## Release gates

The milestone's acceptance is the roadmap's: token overhead falls while
recovery accuracy holds. Fewer calls with lower accuracy is a failure, not a
saving, and so is a smaller input bought with more cache writes than it saves.

| Gate | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| WP0 channel | The inherited 0.5.3 criterion judged, unchanged, on a channel meeting M3, with denominators; or recorded as not met on a qualifying channel, for the maintainer |
| Report | Every candidate run carries all WP1 columns, cache writes and `usageMissing` included; the offline report regressions pass and the baseline shape is recorded |
| Reduction | All WP2 regressions pass; offline probes on reduced sessions score as without reduction; on the provider runs, net token overhead (cache writes and page-backs charged) falls and recovery accuracy holds |
| Rendering | Stored summaries byte-identical; per-request projection tokens fall for an accepted candidate; recovery accuracy holds on the probes |
| Retrieval | Accuracy per component no lower than the baseline, calls and retrieval tokens per question no higher; unneeded excerpts reported; a candidate that trades accuracy for calls fails |
| Compatibility | Strict stock-Pi acceptance and scenarios pass on Pi 1.3.0, 1.2.0 and 1.1.0; v0.4.x and v0.5.x checkpoints reopen; the long-session request-overhead target holds with reduction on and off |
| Identity | `verify` reports no change to the resident `build.ts` and no `EVAL_FROZEN_INPUT_CHANGED` |

Evidence discipline follows [operations.md](operations.md#evidence). Synthetic
and scripted evidence — the regressions, scenario checks, loopback bench and
report runs — is designed now and runs offline under `PI_OFFLINE=1` with
`EVAL_MODEL_CALL_BUDGET=0`; it establishes mechanics and report arithmetic,
never provider quality. Provider evidence is deferred: each bench is its own
maintainer-budgeted step under M3 and M4, run side by side with its baseline on
the same day, at least twice per build, and recorded in `validation.md` with
denominators and failures as they occurred. None runs as part of this plan.

## Deferred and out of scope

- **No host-neutral refactor in 0.6.0.** That is 0.7.0. This plan only requires
  the pure-function shape (G1) so the lift needs no behavior change.
- **No change to the resident runtime, its build fingerprint, the checkpoint
  schema, the stored-checkpoint rendering or the evaluation pin.** The frozen
  F-series fixtures and `eval/runtime.ts` do not move.
- **No second history database, no persisted reduction, index or view.** Every
  projection is recomputed from the records; persisting one is a scope decision
  that only measurement can raise, and it would still not be a history store.
- **No substitute writer, and failure blocking stays.** A reduction never
  stands in for a failed compaction in 0.6.0; the opt-in fallback is evaluated
  in 0.8.0, after these gates, by the maintainer.
- **No rewriting of session files**, no shrinking of stored entries in place,
  no deletion of originals.
- **No provider-enforced output formats**; official Pi does not expose them to
  an extension, and answers that are not JSON stay with the guard.
- **No model call in any reduction, eligibility test, rendering or excerpt
  decision.** Model judgment stays offline, on samples, never in the path.
- **The broader instruction-authority protocol** keeps its own release; WP3
  carries only the role-rendering first step, under M7.
- **Delegated retrieval and canonical source hashes** stay unassigned, as the
  roadmap records; the 0.5.0 exclusions (SQLite/FTS5, embeddings, reflector-
  style rewriting, cross-task memory) stand.

## External references

These candidates follow the community work the roadmap already names; the
descriptions below restate the roadmap's survey and claim no new review.

| Project | Followed here | Not followed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Better Compact | Deterministic reduction tiers without a model call; a tool call and its result treated as one unit (WP2, G5) | Shrinking stored entries in place; the session file is never rewritten |
| Oh My Pi | Touch an entry only when the suffix it invalidates is short, or after an idle gap (WP2 timing) | Its background compaction; it is a modified host and a 0.8.0 baseline, not a mechanism here |

## Open points, left to implementation

- The exact reducible tool set for R-B and the deterministic definition of a
  completed turn after the result; settled against the P4 fixture and recorded
  in `reduction.ts`.
- The identifier pattern and the secret-filter pattern list for WP4, each fixed
  in its module with regressions; planted-secret cases decide the list.
- The excerpt bounds (count and tokens per request), proposed above, confirmed
  by the unneeded-excerpt column.
- The one-line stub's exact wording, byte-stable and free of anything
  time-dependent.
- How the bench marks a reduced session beside an unreduced control so the WP1
